Good to know

Questions people ask

Can you fix it while I wait?

Same day service on most repairs. Small jobs like a watch battery are often done in minutes. Bigger jobs: ask at the counter.

Do I need an appointment?

Not for repairs. Walk-ins are welcome. Custom work is by appointment.

Do you restring pearls?

Yes. Pearl restringing is one of the shop's specialties.

Do you do watch batteries and bands?

Yes. Batteries, band sizing and small adjustments.

Can you set a stone or make something custom?

Yes. Diamond and gemstone setting, and custom design by appointment.

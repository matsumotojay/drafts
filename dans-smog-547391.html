Questions drivers ask

Good to know

What's a STAR station?

Some DMV renewal notices say the car has to be tested at a STAR station. STAR stations meet extra state performance standards. The STAR Certified sign is up at Dan's, so a STAR notice is fine here.

What if my car doesn't pass?

You get the test report. Have the repair done at any shop you like, then come back for the retest. Dan's promise is pass or free retest within 30 days?confirm. The state's Consumer Assistance Program can help pay for repairs if you qualify.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment necessary. Pull in during open hours. Weekdays tend to be quicker than Saturdays.

The guru

Guru Deepali Vora

Founder and director of Nityashetra. Teacher, dancer, choreographer and nattuvanar. She trained in the Thanjavur style of Bharatanatyam in Chennai, and started out as an assistant teacher to her own guru before opening her school here.

Questions drivers ask

Good to know

What's a STAR station?

Some DMV renewal notices say the car has to be tested at a STAR station. STAR stations meet extra state performance standards. Beach Smog has the STAR Certified tag on its state sign, so a STAR notice is fine here.

What if my car doesn't pass?

You get the test report. Have the repair done at any shop you like, then come back for the retest. Free retest?tell us if you offer one The state's Consumer Assistance Program can help pay for repairs if you qualify.

Do I need an appointment?

Best to call ahead for a time: (310) 372-8911. Customers often get in the same day.

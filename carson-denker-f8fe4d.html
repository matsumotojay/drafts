How it works

Pull in, pay, wash, go

1

Pull into an open bay

Drive in and park under the canopy. It can get busy, so off hours are quickest.

2

Pay at the meter

Quarters, dollar coins, or a credit or debit card. Card readers take tap to pay too.

3

Turn the knob

Pick a cycle: tire cleaner, presoak, soap, foam brush, rinse and more. Add money before time runs out to keep going.

4

Dry and vacuum

Use the air dryer in the bay, then pull out to the lot. Plenty of room to dry off and vacuum.

The cycle board

Ten cycles on every bay

Same order as the board on the wall. Turn the knob to switch any time.
